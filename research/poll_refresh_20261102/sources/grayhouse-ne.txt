METHODOLOGY
Interview Dates October 29-November 1, 2026
Target Population Likely voters in Nebraska
Sampling Method Voter-file sample of registered voters in Nebraska;
interviews completed by SMS text-to-web and live
telephone calls to cell phones and landlines.
Weighting Weighted to reflect the modeled likely November
2026 electorate in Nebraska. The weights balance
gender, age, race, region, urbanicity, party registration
and 2024 presidential vote (Trump 56%, Harris 40%).
Final weights are calibrated to GrayHouse's political
engagement model, so that voters across the full
range of turnout propensity are represented in their
expected share of the electorate.
Counts shown in tables are rounded weighted counts;
percentages and breakouts are weighted.
Number of respondents 534
Margin of error +/- 4.2% at 95% confidence
Survey Modes SMS text-to-web: 60% (n=318); Live-to-cell: 31%
(n=166); Live-to-landline: 9% (n=50).
