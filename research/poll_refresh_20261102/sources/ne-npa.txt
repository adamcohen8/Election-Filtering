BOTTOM LINE
In the final week of the campaign, Republican Pete Ricketts leads Independent Dan Osborn 48% to
45% for U.S. Senate, within the +/-3.5% margin of error.
U.S. SENATE: RICKETTS LEADS BY 3
Statewide likely voters (n=800). Ballot totals include voters leaning toward a candidate. The analysis below is based on Full Ballot
crosstabs.
• Ricketts leads by 3. Asked the full five-candidate ballot, voters favor Pete Ricketts (R) 48% to
Dan Osborn (I) 45%, with the three minor-party candidates at 4% combined and 3% undecided.
Head-to-head, it is 49% to 48%.
• Undecided and minor-party voters lean Republican. 7% of likely voters are undecided or
backing a minor-party candidate on the full ballot. This group is 54% registered Republican and
19% registered Democrat, and 66% voted for Donald Trump in 2024.
