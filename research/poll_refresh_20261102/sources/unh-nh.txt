Granite State Poll Methodology
These findings are based on the latest Granite State Poll, conducted by the University of New Hampshire Survey Center. Granite State
Panel members were sent an invita(cid:415)on to complete the survey and 1,857 completed the survey online, yielding a response rate of 39%.
In addi(cid:415)on, 325 New Hampshire residents completed the survey a(cid:332)er receiving an invita(cid:415)on via text message to complete the survey.
Overall, 2,182 New Hampshire residents completed the survey online between October 27 and October 31, 2026. The margin of
sampling error for the survey is +/- 2.1 percent. Included in the sample were 2,040 likely general elec(cid:415)on voters (margin of sampling
error +/- 2.2 percent), 1,032 of whom reside in New Hampshire's First Congressional District (MOE +/- 3.1 percent) and 1,005 of whom
reside in New Hampshire's Second Congressional District (MOE +/- 3.1 percent). In addi(cid:415)on to poten(cid:415)al sampling error, all surveys have
other poten(cid:415)al sources of non-sampling error including ques(cid:415)on order effects, ques(cid:415)on wording effects, and non-response. These
MSE's have not been adjusted for design effect. The design effect for the survey is 2.4%.
