Fieldwork YouGov
Interviewing Dates October 26-29, 2026
Target population Likely Voters in Iowa
Sample Stratified random sample of voters selected from a list of
Iowa registered voters who were contacted via text mes-
sageandinterviewedonYouGov’sweb-basedsurveysys-
tem. Voters were selected at random within each stratum
until the target number of interviews were completed.
Weighting The sample was weighted to gender, age, in-state ge-
ography (using the current Iowa voter list), party reg-
istration, and imputed education and 2024 presidential
vote. Weights were computed using the method of rak-
ing. Likely voters are defined using a model that takes
into account past vote, demographics, and self-reported
likelihoodtovote. Thelikelyvoterweightsrangefromless
than 0.1 to 10.4 with a coefficient of variation of 1.0.
Number of respondents 2,020
Margin of error ± 3.1% (2,020 Iowa Likely Voters)
