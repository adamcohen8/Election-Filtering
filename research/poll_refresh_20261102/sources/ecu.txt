Reading the tables
Each section reports column percentages rounded to the nearest whole number. Percentages are calculated within the column categories shown. Totals may be slightly higher or lower than 100% because of rounding. Positive
percentages that round to 0% are shown as <1%.
Results use raked weights. Weighted cell counts are rounded before calculating percentages.
Q5r combines candidate preferences among respondents who had not yet voted with reported votes from respondents who had already voted.
