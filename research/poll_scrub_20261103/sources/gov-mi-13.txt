Key Findings
Benson holds a +9-point lead in the Governor's race: Polling conducted by Concord Public Opinion Partners of likely 2026 General Election voters in Michigan shows Democrat Jocelyn Benson leading Republican John James by +9 points (50% Benson / 41% James), with 3% choosing someone else and 5% undecided.
El-Sayed leads by +10 points in the Senate race: Democrat Abdul El-Sayed leads Republican Mike Rogers by +10 points (51% El-Sayed / 41% Rogers), with 4% choosing someone else and 4% undecided.
Republicans are unpopular with general election voters: President Trump has a -18-point net favorability rating (39% favorable / 57% unfavorable). Rogers has a -14-point net favorability rating (40% favorable / 54% unfavorable), and James has a -11-point net favorability rating (39% favorable / 50% unfavorable).
Democrats for statewide office are viewed more positively: Benson has a +11-point net favorability rating (50% favorable / 39% unfavorable), and El-Sayed has a +6-point net favorability rating (51% favorable / 45% unfavorable).
Methodology
This survey was conducted between 10/30–11/01 with a sample size of 510 respondents. Data was collected by contacting likely general election voters via live calls to cell phones and landlines and text-to-web surveys. The margin of error for this survey is ±4.3%. Data was weighted by Race, Age, Gender, Partisanship, College Attainment, CD, and Mode. Conducted by Concord Public Opinion Partners for Education Reform Now Advocacy.
