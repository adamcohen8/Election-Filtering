About Us

Overview

InsiderAdvantage has numerous media related products ranging from its national public opinion polling to online and print products including the very popular Georgia-based political and business publication James Magazine.

Public Opinion Polling
InsiderAdvantage began surveying public opinion in 2000. Over the years InsiderAdvantage has polled for television affiliates across the nation ranging from ABC, NBC, and Fox affiliates in major TV markets to print and online publications such as Politico and Newsmax. In the latest RealClearPolitics pollster accountability ratings, among pollsters who poll multiple states in presidential and midterm elections, InsiderAdvantage is ranked in the top ten of all pollsters nationwide and ahead of all cable and network news polling and, with the exception of The New York Times,  all national newspapers and publications. In the last presidential election cycle InsiderAdvantage was ranked number one in accuracy by RealClearPolitics. Source, RealClearPolitics.
Polling Operations

InsiderAdvantage’s polling operations are located in Florida. For just one example of our record, and our on-air analysis, watch this edition of FOX13 Tampa’s Money, Power, Politics Show.

Meet Our Co-Founder & Chairman

Matt Towery is the co-founder and Chairman of InsiderAdvantage/Internet News Agency. He is a graduate of England’s Cambridge University (Magdalene College) and Florida’s Stetson University College of Law. Mr. Towery is the author of four books and wrote a nationally syndicated column (Creators Syndicate) for 15 years until he began his semi-retirement in Saint Petersburg, FL. Mr. Towery has served as a political analyst for network and cable news programs since 2000 and has served as one of the on-air pollsters for the Sean Hannity Show on radio since 2017.

Mr. Towery is an AV preeminent rated attorney and remains of counsel with the national law firm of Hall, Booth, Smith. He continues to supervise polling for InsiderAdvantage and writes for, and is Chairman of, the popular Georgia magazine James. He created and launched the magazine in 2004.

Mr. Towery served in the Georgia House of Representatives in the 1990s. In 1998, the Georgia legislature passed, and the late Governor Zell Miller (D) signed into law, the naming of the Paces Ferry bridge after Mr. Towery. It abuts Home Depot’s worldwide headquarters in Metro Atlanta.

Mr. Towery co-hosts the newly created podcast Polling Plus with fellow pollster Robert Cahaly. He now lives in Saint Petersburg with his wife Dolle. He has become an avid golfer in his years since taking semi-retirement.
