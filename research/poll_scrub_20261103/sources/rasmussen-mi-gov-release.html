Michigan Governor: Democrat Benson Has 10-Point Lead

Friday, October 30, 2026

Michigan’s Democratic Attorney General Jocelyn Benson leads by double digits over Republican Rep. John James in the race to be the state’s next governor.

The latest Rasmussen Reports national telephone and online survey finds that, if the election were held today, 50% of Likely Michigan voters would vote for Benson, while 40% would vote for James. Libertarian candidate  Anthony Hudson, U.S. Taxpayers Party candidate Donna Brandenburg, and Green Party candidate Douglas Campbell each have one percent (1%) support, while seven percent (7%) are undecided.
