What GOTV Polling Does
Fast polls of real voters,
broken down every way that matters.
One response per phone, matched to the voter file, published with the method on every page.
Three Ways to Field
Text-to-web surveys, text reply polls and automated phone (IVR) surveys of registered voters. One response per phone, matched to the voter file.
Mapped to the Precinct
Results by county, district and precinct, with any group under 30 responses held back.
Crosstabs on Every Question
Party, age, sex, vote history and geography on every question, explorable on the page and ready to copy.
Same-Day Fielding
Questions in the morning, responses coming in the same day when scope allows.
