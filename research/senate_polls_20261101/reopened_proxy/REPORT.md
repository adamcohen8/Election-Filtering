# Reopened party-proxy research

Reviewed all 12 original race inventories for modeled classification, model-produced subgroup estimates, missing bases and inaccessible Rasmussen tables. Eight new full six-cell records are in polls.json: two Texas modeled-source records and six previously missing Rasmussen records for Texas (two), Michigan (two), Iowa and Ohio. Evidence includes the original two Texas PDFs and six official Rasmussen HTML pages. NC ActiVote and OH VoteHub/Verasight were already present in prior race polls and are not duplicated; Quantus is being handled separately. Other ActiVote reports lacked a party ballot table in prior inventories, and CNN DocumentCloud reports remained inaccessible.

A2 estimates (TX, fielded Oct 23–27) are output from a fitted statistical model, with party leaners assigned to their leaned major party and pure independents separate. Reported turnout proportions are also modeled; actual subgroup Ns are unknown. Exact release date is not independently confirmed, but October 29 public reporting establishes that it was released by then and on or after Oct 27 fieldwork, within the authorized window. Preserve the date interval instead of representing October 28 as established. Pollfinity estimates are observed weighted candidate responses grouped by modeled voter-file party, not model-produced candidate responses. It reports nonpartisan n=17. Although its cover calls the sample registered, its own S1 table says 5% were not registered; retain the conflict. Pollster confirms Oct 22 release.

Rasmussen labels its group columns Party, but does not disclose in these tables how respondents are classified or whether party leaners are reassigned. Ns are null because exact subgroup counts are unreported; rounded composition shares are recorded separately. Dates in prior inventory, contemporary reporting, and Michigan release index supply publication-date evidence. Independent/Other is combined except Ohio, where another-party voters form a separate group.

Rasmussen Alaska and Minnesota are already present in race files: direct official Alaska table says Sullivan/Peltola among Independents 43/41, while the article reverses these; the Minnesota October 21 table reports Tafoya 5% among Democrats and Flanagan 6% among Republicans while the article reverses them. A current review confirmed both conflicts. Direct table values are preserved in existing rows; no duplicate observations added. The Alaska table also labels Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. a Republican while other records list no party, and the article wrongly assigns Peltola’s House service to incumbent Sullivan. These source errors are material and cannot be resolved by guessing.
