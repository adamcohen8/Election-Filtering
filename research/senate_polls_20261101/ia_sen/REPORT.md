# Iowa U.S. Senate poll research, publication through November 1, 2026

Search covered Call the Map, 270toWin, PollCheck, Wikipedia's linked archive, Political.org, pollster releases, targeted searches for original party tables, and public workbooks. The inventory records 20 identified candidates including two explicitly out of scope; completeness of the open internet cannot be proven. Publication date governs inclusion. Ashley Hinson (R) and Josh Turek (D) are the matched candidates; Thomas Laehn (L) is a listed third candidate in several ballots.

Five independent waves have complete candidate-support by party columns, including one needing substantive review: Fox, CNN/SSRS, Emerson, Marist, and On Point. `polls.json` contains six records because Marist's registered-voter result is a same-wave alternative, never an independent observation. Emerson has reported weighted fractional bases, while Fox, CNN, and Marist likely voters lack usable subgroup n. Fox and CNN disclose minimum unweighted sample sizes of 100 and 125 respectively for displayed subgroups, not actual counts. Marist's RV alternative has estimates formed from 1,100 respondents times rounded weighted composition; these are not unweighted counts. Marist's 1,100 cannot be used as the likely-voter base. Marist publication is listed Oct 15 by 270toWin and retrieved Oct 14 on another archive, both in the window.

On Point's Oct 21 release links its complete workbook. Its first-choice LV ballot is recorded before modeled allocation of undecideds. Its workbook labels the relevant columns party ID, while the accompanying text refers to registered no-party voters; classification and leaning need confirmation. The workbook also labels 171/204/84 as LV unweighted subgroup bases, yet repeats the RV bases exactly and describes the LV universe as about 327 of 491. These are preserved as reported, flagged for review, and should not be treated as confirmed LV subgroup sizes. Release narrative describes fieldwork Oct 19–21 whereas workbook states Oct 20–21; primary workbook dates retained with conflict disclosed.

The CNN PDF was viewed in the DocumentCloud browser text viewer, including pages 1 and 10; direct web and ordinary download attempts returned 403. A brief transcription is preserved. GOTV's live poll suppresses its nonpartisan group under 30; its candidate cells are unknown, not zero. Other exclusions include YouGov's two polls (published Senate crossbreaks lack party), the NPA memo (only R and D), Trafalgar (ballot crossbreaks without party), NetChoice/Echelon (state toplines), InsiderAdvantage (tabs posting problem; site directs to X), Suffolk marginals, co/efficient toplines, inaccessible Cook and NYT tables, Rasmussen 404, and Quantus released September 30. Wedgewood is fabricated and explicitly excluded. The poll inventory supplies exact references, details, and field-date discrepancies.
