# North Carolina Senate backfill

Three distinct pre-October releases with complete candidate-by-party cells are preserved in `polls.json`, in descending field-end order. All are review candidates, not changes to the model or central ingestion decisions. Percentages below are Whatley / Cooper within each subgroup, not shares among candidate voters.

| Field dates | Poll | R | D | Independent or unaffiliated | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 17–20 | Opinion Diagnostics / Common Cause | 88 / 7 | 3 / 95 | 32 / 53 | Self-reported registration; weighted bases 290 / 282 / 282 |
| Sep 13–15 | Harper / Carolina Journal | 74.0485 / 4.4080 | 1.4526 / 89.7545 | 29.7968 / 51.3831 | Self-reported registration; weighted rounded bases 199 / 203 / 174 |
| Aug 31–Sep 3 | ECU | 79 / 11 | 1 / 85 | 34 / 45 | Self-described party ID; subgroup bases unavailable |

Opinion Diagnostics' [original 24-page report](https://www.commoncause.org/north-carolina/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CCNC-Sep-2026-Poll-Release-1.pdf), p. 13, gives initial ballot rows and p. 9 identifies self-reported registration and dates. The [September 25 sponsor release](https://www.commoncause.org/north-carolina/press/2026_poll_1/) confirms timing and population. Unaffiliated registration is mapped to independent, while other parties are separate. The separately reported leaner-allocated ballot is not used.

Harper's [original workbook](https://www.carolinajournal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/H24747-CJ-NC-Sept26-Crosstabs.xlsx), Tables sheet rows 9004–9035, supplies the six cells and rounded weighted bases. The [toplines](https://www.carolinajournal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/H24747-CJ-NC-Sept26-Toplines.pdf), cover and Q29, establish dates, population and the explicit registration question; the [release](https://www.carolinajournal.com/polls/september-2026-roy-coopers-lead-grows-to-15-points-in-nc-senate-race/) is dated Sep 17. Other and unsure registrations are separate. Raw underlying precision is preserved; it exceeds the displayed rounding.

ECU's [original crosstabs](https://cdn.filestackcontent.com/obyggT8TXCZjJrGUQNgk), p. 2, report party identification by initial Senate choice; [ECU's September 10 release](https://surveyresearch-ecu.reportablenews.com/pr/ecu-poll-cooper-leads-whatley-by-7-points) confirms dates, population, identification and distinction from forced choice. Its 1% Whatley among Democrats is a reported whole percentage, and subgroup counts are null.

## Newer and intervening unresolved candidates

These three are the latest **verified here**, not a claim that no newer qualifying wave exists. [Big Data Poll](https://www.bigdatapoll.com/blog/tar-heel-state-poll-roy-cooper-holds-solid-lead-over-michael-whatley-for-us-senate-in-north-carolina/) fielded Sep 27–29, published Sep 30: the linked [MarketSight](https://application.marketsight.com/app/ItemView.aspx?SharedBy=34090&SharedFor=public%40bigdatapoll.com&id=58a7b43c-45f1-4819-8142-b4d501839b4d) opens to the NC page but the six ballot cells on later interactive pages were not verified. The release methodology says Texas, while the NC heading and MarketSight weighting identify North Carolina; the discrepancy is retained. The [Catawba Sep 30 release](https://www.catawba.edu/news/all-news/2026/yougov-39/) gives four of six cells for Sep 4–15; its linked table redirects to sign-in. Neither missing crossover can be inferred.

Quantus Sep 21–22, AARP Sep 17–20, InsiderAdvantage Sep 16–17, and YouGov Sep 15–18 are potentially more recent; complete six-cell accessible original tables were not verified in this search. [YouGov's original report](https://d3nkl3psvxxpe9.cloudfront.net/documents/ttw_nc_20260915.pdf) includes demographic and turnout-model tables for Senate choice but no party cross-tab. [High Point's memo](https://www.highpoint.edu/src/files/2023/08/HPUPoll127MemoA-1.pdf) for Sep 6–16 gives the topline, but no party ballot table was located. [Elon's charts](https://eloncdn.blob.core.windows.net/eu3/sites/819/2026/09/Elon-University-Poll-charts-9-10-26.pdf) are toplines. These gaps could displace Harper or ECU if complete qualified tables are later verified.
