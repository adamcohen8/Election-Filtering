# Michigan Senate polling research, 1 October–1 November 2026

The search found five waves with usable three-group candidate crosstabs: RedBox, Data for Progress, On Point, Marist, and CNN/SSRS. Three have reported subgroup bases (RedBox and On Point unweighted; Data for Progress weighted); Marist LV and CNN/SSRS lack exact group bases. Marist RV is supplied as an alternate for review, sharing its LV sample ID and never to be counted in addition. Exact Marist publication date was not independently established; the Wikipedia citation says retrieved October 20, after the October 18 field end, so the release was available in the window but the JSON date remains null. All rows remain subject to central review.

The Marist RV alternate has n estimates 379, 414, 343 (Republican, Democrat, Independent) calculated by flooring 1,185 times the reported rounded weighted composition. These are estimates and not reported interview counts. Its LV figures are weighted across the registered sample, so a nominal LV respondent total should not be inferred. Marist suppresses groups below 50 unweighted; CNN/SSRS suppresses groups below 125 unweighted. Those thresholds give only lower bounds for displayed groups, not exact counts. SSRS's vote table combines initial support and leaners, and its Independent/Other group combines categories. Its 768 registered voters were weighted to likelihood of voting. Party question wording and lean classification need confirmation.

RedBox's Independent column means respondents who did not identify as Democrat or Republican, including other party and don't know, and is not the separately tabulated 'true independents' subset. Data for Progress labels its intermediate group Independent/Third party, and explicitly states party affiliation is self-identified. On Point's first-choice crosstab is read from the exported, public workbook, not its subsequently modeled leaner allocation. Its sample uses all 586 registered respondents as the unweighted base for the likely-voter weighted table; the workbook calls the field preliminary/partial. The party question wording/lean treatment was not established. Poll values are weighted percentages; subgroup bases retain their source designations.

Coverage included Wikipedia, RCP, On Point, 270toWin and Polling Desk, plus original polling reports and release pages. Inventory enumerates discovered candidate waves and limitations. The Oct 1 Trafalgar release is eligible by publication even though its fieldwork was in September; its report has age, gender and race crosstabs but no party-vote crosstab. YouGov's Sep 22–Oct 4 Michigan report likewise lacks party in its vote table. RCP's link for its Oct 26–29 YouGov Michigan item actually goes to a Texas PDF. Guidant's NYT PDF was not available through the web tool; MSU/Verasight's page returned 403. The CNN DocumentCloud PDF initially returned 403 through web retrieval, but its original pages 1 and 11 were subsequently read in the browser; no full PDF download is claimed. Other waves are listed as exclusions or inaccessible because the complete party vote table was not established, not because a negative finding is exhaustive. Wedgewood is explicitly excluded per assignment.
