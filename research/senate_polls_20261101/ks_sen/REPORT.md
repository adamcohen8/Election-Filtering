# Kansas Senate poll research — 1 November 2026

Search covered the October 1–November 1 inclusive publication window, including September fieldwork, via 535, RealClearPolling, 270toWin, PollCheck, Ballotpedia, Wikipedia, pollster releases, news reports, and targeted crosstab searches (queries and URLs in inventory.json). This is a documented broad search, not proof of exhaustive coverage. The research records three releases with all six party-group ballot cells: Emerson, Washington Post/Schar, and RedBox. Emerson is ready with reported weighted denominators; Washington Post/Schar needs denominators; RedBox is retained for source review. The Democrat is Adam Hamilton and the Republican is Roger Marshall across these releases.

* **Emerson, October 30**: Original workbook, `crosstabs`, rows 4–8, records first-choice vote by **self-reported registration**, not self-described partisan ID. Republican/independent-or-other/Democrat weighted counts are approximately 412.766/117.447/269.787, respectively. They are weighted quantities, not counts of interviews. The Independent/other category includes other registrations. Undecided-leaner follow-up is a separate ballot and is not used.
* **Washington Post/Schar, October 30**: Original workbook, `Kansas likely voters`, rows 1–3 and 11–16, displays first-choice three-candidate vote by registration. Its non-D/R column is labeled “All others,” encompassing unaffiliated and minor-party registration; subgroup interviews or shares are not reported in the workbook. Margins of error cannot establish exact bases. `needs_denominator` preserves results without imputing counts. The source explicitly reports registered independents in [its accompanying article](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/30/we-polled-kansas-democrats-wanted-talk-republicans-not-so-much/).
* **RedBox for A Fight Worth Having, October 25**: Original CSV supplies full unrounded weighted percentages and unweighted party-ID bases of 406 Republicans, 455 Democrats and 440 Independents. Q2/Q3 combines actual votes with prospective first choices; no candidate leaners were allocated. The release defines Independents as respondents who did not call themselves Democrats or Republicans, regardless of registration. Use this self-ID banner rather than a second count of its voter-file banner. A [Reddit commenter](https://www.reddit.com/r/fivethirtyeight/comments/1xfmp5n/a_fight_worth_having_dredbox_research_kansas_poll/) alleged the pollster was probably not real; this is an unverified allegation and is **not** proof of fabrication. The sponsor explicitly describes RedBox as its polling unit and publishes its method and tables. `needs_review` signals the unresolved concern, not a finding of fraud. This differs from Wedgewood, explicitly identified in the assignment as fabricated.
