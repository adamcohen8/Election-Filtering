# Kansas Senate pre-October backfill — 1 November 2026

Two qualifying publication waves were located, leaving the requested three-wave backfill one short. Search proceeded backward from September 30; no claim of exhaustive coverage is made. Both records provide all six conditional Marshall/Hamilton shares. They do not establish that no newer qualifying table exists: unresolved candidate waves are listed below and in `inventory.json`. Existing October-window findings are separate.

The Tavern Hamilton-as-independent and informed questions are variants of the same sample and are not counted as separate waves. The unverified search gaps between these two waves matter for a latest-first selection: co/efficient's September 8–10 original release provides ballot toplines and party registration composition but no conditional party ballot table; the August 12–16 GSG/People Over Politics, August 7–8 PPP, and July 8–13 GBAO surveys lack all six cells. The [August 13 PPP release](https://www.publicpolicypolling.com/polls/a-toss-up-in-kansas/) gives independent Hamilton 57% and Marshall 26% and Republican Hamilton 14%, but neither Republican Marshall nor the Democratic group cells. GQR April 23–27 was a Patrick Schmidt matchup despite one aggregator's Hamilton label. GSG's September 13–16 survey appears in an October 2 attorney-general memo; its Senate publication date before the cutoff and the six cells remain unverified. September Wedgewood is fabricated and excluded.

NYT/Siena was published October 3 and is outside this backfill; prior research found its [crosstabs](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/10/03/polls/times-siena-kansas-poll-crosstabs.html) blocked by browser policy. The [official 2026 Kansas Speaks report](https://www.fhsu.edu/docking/Kansas-Speaks/2026-kansas-speaks-survey-report_final.pdf), published October 26, was inspected under the expanded instruction to consider missing October waves. Its 217 pages include the questionnaire and party crosstabs: Q19_3 (printed p. 100) concerns satisfaction with Marshall, not vote choice, and Q23 (printed p. 54) asks about the governor race. The report contains no Hamilton reference or Senate ballot question. It cannot supply six candidate-support cells. The report is saved under `sources/`. No blocked site was retrieved by alternate means. The JSON retains missing subgroup denominators as null and the January release date as a bound, not an invented date.

Targeted searches of Fort Hays/Docking, prior Emerson Kansas surveys, Data for Progress, McLaughlin, Tavern, SurveyUSA, Rasmussen and the principal polling aggregators identified no additional verified six-cell Marshall–Hamilton wave. This limits the search result, not the existence of private, unindexed, or unreleased tables.
