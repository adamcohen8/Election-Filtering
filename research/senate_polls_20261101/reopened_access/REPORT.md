# Reopened polling sources

Recovered five complete party-by-candidate tables using the ordinary Safari viewer: four CNN/SSRS surveys in Texas, Maine, Kansas and Ohio, plus Bowling Green State University/YouGov Ohio. Each source and table location is preserved in polls.json and source excerpts.

The four SSRS surveys sample registered voters and create likely-voter results by weighting every registered respondent by voting probability. Their sample N is therefore retained separately as registered-voter N, with subgroup bases and LV N null. Their 3-way party table combines Independent and Other; vote measures include candidate leaners. SSRS publishes a minimum subgroup size of 125 unweighted cases, not the exact bases. BGSU reports unweighted bases and uses party ID with leaners.

NYT/Siena prior attempts encountered a site-safety gate; these were not bypassed. Quantus and modeled-party/Rasmussen assignments were handled by other researchers. AJC and Mason-Dixon were already recovered centrally and were not duplicated. Research is targeted, not a proof of exhaustive coverage.
