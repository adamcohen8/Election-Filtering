# Georgia U.S. Senate polling research, October 1–November 1, 2026

**Result: one candidate observation for review.** Five identified genuine October releases were Trafalgar, Quantus, ActiVote, University of Georgia/AJC, and Echelon/NetChoice. AJC reports candidate support by three-point party category, but the underlying party question and treatment of leaners are not stated; its estimated subgroup bases derive from rounded weighted shares. The other four did not furnish a qualifying party crosstab in the materials inspected. Wedgewood is separately excluded as a disclosed fabricated pollster. The AJC row is marked `needs_review`.

Search covered the listed aggregators, news releases, primary reports, and queries recorded in `inventory.json`; it cannot prove all public releases were found. The date rule is publication date, not field end. Three September releases encountered are retained in the inventory as out-of-window examples. Within a wave, alternative ballot versions are not separate surveys. The AJC subgroup bases are derived estimates, not exact reported sample sizes.

- **Quantus, October 19**, fielded October 15–17: its [31-page report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1e2r7nm_omQkzU9N5XySW6BidnadoY3vB/view) was read in the public Google Drive viewer. Page 10 labels its full-sample, initial-ballot table “PARTY ID (MODELED),” while page 3 explains party is a mapped voter-file field. This is an inferred voter-file proxy, not demonstrated self-reported party identity or actual registration. We exclude it despite the complete table, whose cells and bases are preserved in `sources/quantus_transcription.txt`; the table does not establish whether its base n values are weighted or unweighted. The PDF itself was not downloaded.
- **AJC/UGA, Senate story October 21**, fielded October 7–15: the [public DocumentCloud text viewer](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28824163-ajc-poll-october-2026-1/?mode=text) has the Senate table on p. 4 and Political Party (3pt) composition on p. 29. Among Republicans, Collins 85.8%, Ossoff 4.8%; among Democrats, Collins 0.9%, Ossoff 94.8%; among Independents, Collins 30.2%, Ossoff 49.2%. Total 1,000 likely voters; reported weighted party shares 38.0%, 35.6%, 26.4%, respectively, yield estimated bases 380, 356, 264. The exact wording and inclusion of leaners are not given; Georgia does not register voters by party, so the party-ID interpretation is inferred and marked for review. The [Senate article](https://www.ajc.com/politics/2026/10/ajc-poll-ossoff-leads-collins-in-georgias-us-senate-race/) was published October 21, while [governor coverage](https://cmg-cmg-tv-10010-prod.web.arc-cdn.net/news/local/ajcuga-poll-bottoms-jackson-dead-heat-georgia-governor-race/76TN3NROXRG4HDILVYDUMJS4RQ/) appeared October 20; the PDF cover is dated October 16 and viewer creation metadata October 19, neither establishing earliest public availability. Source transcription: `sources/ajc_transcription.txt`.
- **Trafalgar, October 10**, fielded October 7–9: [original report](https://www.thetrafalgargroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GA-General-Report-1010.pdf) has candidate-support tables for age, gender and ethnicity (pp. 8–9), but no party-support crosstab. Party composition alone does not supply the missing cells.
- **ActiVote, October 20**, fielded October 6–20: [pollster’s report](https://www.activote.net/polls/2026/ga-u-s-senate/) explicitly says it does not report party affiliation in Georgia. Its left/center/right spectrum is not party identification. The Hill lists Oct 7–21, while the original pollster states Oct 6–20.
- **Echelon/NetChoice, October 27**, fielded October 16–23: the [original topline](https://netchoice.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/NetChoice-October-2026-Antitrust-Tech-Survey-Topline.pdf) Q12 p. 7 reports state totals only, not candidate choice by party group. It lists Libertarian Allen Buckley at 3%, although [Political.org](https://political.org/2026-elections/) says he was not on the ballot; this is a question-composition issue, not a candidate-name conflict for the modeled nominees. DDHQ lists Oct 17–24, while the original report states Oct 16–23.
- **Wedgewood**, fielded October 2–3: excluded regardless of crosstabs. The [aggregator](https://polls.politicalbetting.com/pollsters/wedgewood-polls) identifies the wave, and an October 4 [forecast changelog](https://onpointpolitics.us/forecast/changelog) establishes it had appeared by then; precise publication date is unverified.

The major-party matchup throughout the qualifying searches is Republican Mike Collins against Democrat Jon Ossoff. No unsupported subgroup sizes, party categories, or candidate-support values have been imputed.
