# Maine Senate poll research, October 1–November 1, 2026

Searched 270toWin, Call the Map, US Polling Data, PollCheck, Ballotpedia, Wikipedia, contemporaneous news, and original pollsters and sponsors. Inventory has 18 releases or candidates including a blocked fabricated firm and one conflicting cutoff case. Eight waves have party-group candidate support transcribed: AARP Oct 1; CBS/YouGov Oct 2; Marist Oct 6; Washington Post/Schar Oct 9; Rasmussen Oct 9; Suffolk Oct 14; Marist Oct 27; Data for Progress Oct 30. Exact references and short transcriptions are in polls.json and sources/. This is a thorough search, not proof that every online release was located.

Two are marked ready (CBS and Data for Progress); Washington Post and late Marist need denominators. Early Marist has estimated counts from reported sample and rounded composition, requiring review. Suffolk registration counts have unspecified weighting and an independent/other proxy. Rasmussen reports party but does not establish how party was measured, so is flagged for review; model-inferred party must not be accepted. For these three records root should evaluate the stated limitations. Late Marist is LV-weighted from 1,449 registered voters: 1,449 is not an LV sample size, despite aggregator labels. WaPo supplies unleaned party ID separately from leaned categories; unleaned only is retained. Other ID is retained separately for Marist, while DFP and WaPo report independent/other together. Suffolk combines 138 unenrolled, 11 Green and 3 Libertarian registrants. No fabricated sample counts.

Direct retrieval obtained AARP and MPRC public PDFs; AARP supplies party candidate support and party composition, while MPRC does not provide the party ballot crosstab. MPRC publication date has conflicting reports. CNN October 29 primary document returned 403 in the web reader. The two Trafalgar reports, Fox, Echelon and November 1 YouGov publish toplines or nonparty crosstabs, without an identified candidate-by-party table. InsiderAdvantage promised separate tabs that were not located. co/efficient dates conflict; its own page says September and has no candidate-by-party breakdown. Wedgewood is excluded as disclosed fabricated. The CBS sample PDF reports 1,144 registered, not the 1,114 stated by some aggregators; the Senate table reports weighted 1,136 LV. Candidate labels Collins and Jackson match sources; Trafalgar calls Jackson Troy Dale Jackson. Poll releases and exact field-end dates are distinguished in inventory.

The AARP PDF (pages 3 and 19) reports 982 likely voters and Party ID R33%, D38%, I29%; the resulting subgroup sizes 324, 373, 284 are estimates from rounded shares, not reported subgroup Ns. Late Marist explicitly suppresses groups under 50 unweighted cases; its displayed R, D, and I groups therefore each have a lower bound of 50, not an exact count or effective sample size. Washington Post workbook was fully inspected: one sheet, 130 rows, with no party counts or shares. Rasmussen’s general methodology describes party weighting but does not establish the individual-level party measure in this wave; historical external discussion cannot establish its current question, so it remains needs review. Suffolk counts remain reported but their weighting is unspecified; any design-factor reduction is an analyst assumption.
