# Reopened Quantus Senate surveys

Six distinct in-window survey waves have complete initial-ballot party-category support tables: Alaska, Georgia, Michigan, North Carolina, and two Texas waves. Original PDFs are preserved in `sources/`. `polls.json` has one row per wave, all flagged for review because the status of the published ballot-table subgroup n (weighted versus unweighted) is not consistently defined, and because some party groups are modeled or have other documented ambiguity. No subgroup base has been invented.

- **Alaska, October 2 release:** September 28–30 fieldwork, initial first-choice Q1 p. 8, 737 race voters from 758 respondents. Sullivan/Peltola among Republicans 87/7 (n=255), Democrats 3/94 (n=139), unaffiliated 34/56 (n=343). Page 3 says party is respondent-reported identification, while p. 4 calls it reported voter-file identification. This inconsistency deserves review. The unaffiliated group is mapped to independent; its relation to other-party respondents is not established. The ballot includes Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. separately from the modeled Republican incumbent Dan S. Sullivan.
- **Michigan, October 7:** October 5–6, p. 9 Q1, 744 race voters from 762 respondents. Rogers/El-Sayed: R 92/3 (n=286), D 4/90 (n=279), I 39/53 (n=179). Party is modeled voter-file identification.
- **Texas, October 13:** October 9–11, p. 9 Q1, 839 race voters from 854 respondents. Paxton/Talarico: R 87/7 (n=376), D 2/95 (n=293), I 37/52 (n=170). Party is modeled from voter file. Brown appears in the ballot but his column is omitted from the demographic table.
- **Georgia, October 19:** October 15–17, p. 10 Q1, 755 likely voters. Collins/Ossoff: R 92/6 (n=290), D 3/96 (n=281), I 35/56 (n=184). Party is modeled voter-file classification.
- **North Carolina, October 26:** October 24–25, p. 10 Q1, 722 race voters. Whatley/Cooper: R 89/7 (n=240), D 3/94 (n=234), unaffiliated 36/55 (n=248). Party is voter-file registration; unaffiliated maps to the model’s independent group. The source does not clarify whether other-party registrations are combined or absent.
- **Texas, October 30:** October 26–28, p. 11 Q2, 1,207 race voters from 1,221 respondents. Paxton/Talarico: R 93/4 (n=563), D 2/96 (n=477), I 33/57 (n=167). Party is modeled voter-file classification. The initial-table n labels do not specify weighting; p. 13 labels the same column counts in the separate derived leaner table weighted equivalents, whereas p. 6 labels full-sample counts unweighted. The Democrat candidate and residual cells exceed 100 by one point after rounding, preserved as reported. Only the initial ballot is ingested.

Iowa’s Quantus release dated September 30 is outside the explicit publication window, notwithstanding any later discussion; it is excluded. The first-choice field-end date, not the publication date, is the observation date. Counts labeled `reported_weighting_unspecified` are published table bases with unresolved status, not asserted unweighted counts. Modeled identification is a voter-file proxy and is not described as respondent self-identification. Source URLs and release dates are documented in `inventory.json` and each observation.
