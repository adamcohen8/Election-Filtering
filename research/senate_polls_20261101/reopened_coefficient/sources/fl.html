Methodology

co/efficient surveyed 1,014 likely general election voters in Florida on October 18-20, 2026, using mobile text responses and landline interviews. Results are weighted on age, gender, race, education, geography, and registered party to reflect the likely 2026 general electorate. The margin of error is ±3.06 percentage points.
