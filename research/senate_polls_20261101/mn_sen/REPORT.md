# Minnesota Senate poll research through November 1, 2026

Minnesota Secretary of State primary results identify Michele Tafoya (Republican) and Peggy Flanagan (Democratic–Farmer–Labor) as the major-party nominees: https://www.sos.mn.gov/elections-voting/election-results/2026/2026-primary-election-results/?searchTerm=RESULTS. AP confirms the general election: https://apnews.com/article/0ad3241b58ecc7fa2ca1693bbd2f4013.

Five distinct released waves contain accessible party candidate crosstabs: SurveyUSA October 13 and 29, Rasmussen October 9 and 22, and Big Data October 2 (fieldwork in September). See polls.json for source transcriptions and inventory.json for search coverage and 14 candidate releases. SurveyUSA's four-candidate table is first choice, whereas Rasmussen reports only the two candidates and unsure. Big Data has an initial ballot and a separate leaner table; initial ballot is retained. Minnesota does not register voters by party; these tables are labeled party identification/affiliation. Precise party question wording and whether leaners are reclassified are not supplied in the documents, so those details remain uncertain.

SurveyUSA reports 718 and 714 likely voters and rounded weighted party composition, but no subgroup counts. The subgroup N values are floor(total × displayed share), estimates and not source-reported counts. Rasmussen independently reports 856 and 1,182 likely voters, both with rounded 32% R / 35% D / 33% I/Other; its source does not disclose subgroup n or exact party question and independent and other are combined. Derived estimates should not be treated as actual counts; both Rasmussen waves are needs_review. For October 21 the source article switches the R/D crossover values relative to its direct table; the direct table is transcribed. Big Data's MarketSight explicitly says Sample Size 215 R / 229 D / 178 I/Other under a weighting variable, but whether those figures are unweighted is not established; it is marked needs_review. Do not treat them as unweighted without confirmation.

Siena's original crosstabs and both NYT browser and escalated curl returned 403; Siena's independent press release establishes the October 28 release. Victory Insights' 12-page campaign-funded report gives vote margins by party but not the two candidate percentages in each group, insufficient for ingestion. Trafalgar has age/gender/race but no party vote table. Other exclusions and unresolved releases are in inventory.json. Wedgewood is blocked as a disclosed fabricated pollster. The poll search is broad but cannot prove completeness.
