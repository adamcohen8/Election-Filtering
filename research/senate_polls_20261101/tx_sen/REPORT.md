# Texas U.S. Senate polling research

The inventory covers 36 identified releases or waves, including the Wedgewood exclusion, and records search queries, sources and exclusions. It covers publication October 1–November 1, 2026, including September fieldwork, but is not proof that every open-internet release was found.

Eleven candidate records are preserved: Houston Hobby, Texas Southern, Marist RV, Data for Progress, Emerson/Nexstar, CBS/YouGov, two On Point waves, UT/Texas Politics, UMass Lowell/YouGov and ReconMR/Siena. Eight have a reported or derived denominator and are marked ready; UT, UMass and ReconMR lack bases. ReconMR combines Independents/other. Marist RV replaces, rather than duplicates, its LV wave: LV reports Democratic support for Paxton as <1% and lacks usable subgroup bases; its interval and values remain metadata. RV bases are floor of 1,196 times rounded published composition, not exact reported n. Houston and Texas Southern bases are similarly estimates from rounded shares.

On Point workbooks provide unweighted subgroup bases but reweight the same registered respondents for likely voters. Their as-asked ballot is retained, not the model-allocated undecided version. Its late workbook title says October 27–28 while the methodology and release say October 27. Emerson reports fractional weighted bases and combines Independent/other. Data for Progress uses self-identified party but combines Independent/third party; its bases are explicitly weighted. CBS includes candidate leaners and gives weighted subgroup bases. These pooled categories are preserved verbatim for central review, not represented as pure independent samples.

A2 report states that subgroup results are produced by a statistical model fitted on party and other characteristics; it is excluded from direct-crosstab ingestion. Early Quantus explicitly labels party ID modeled and is excluded. Later Quantus remains inaccessible and unverified. CNN PDF returned 403 even through direct download; NYT/Siena crosstabs were inaccessible; 254 Labs crosstabs are subscriber-only; TPOR crosstabs are available upon request. Pollfinity uses modeled voter-file party and is excluded. Primary original workbooks/PDFs inspected during the second pass are in sources/. No candidate-name conflict was observed.
