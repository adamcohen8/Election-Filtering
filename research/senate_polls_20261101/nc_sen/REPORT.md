# North Carolina Senate polling research, publication October 1–November 1, 2026

Search covered 270toWin, CallTheMap, Crosstab, ElectionTracker, Wikipedia, pollster releases, linked PDFs and workbooks, and targeted pollster and crosstab searches. Inventory records 11 in-window releases and two exclusions outside the qualifying universe (Catawba published September 30 and the blocked fabricated Wedgewood). Discovery is not proof that all internet releases were found. The Catawba source confirms its September 30 publication even though it was indexed later.

Six waves have party-column ballot results retained in `polls.json`: RedBox (October 25), Change Research (October 13), Harper (October 22), ECU (October 8), SurveyUSA (October 13), and ActiVote (October 20). Three are marked ready for root review and ingestion: RedBox, Change Research, Harper. Three are retained for review only: ECU has censored candidate support and no subgroup bases; SurveyUSA's party affiliation source is not sufficiently defined; ActiVote's party source may mix file-derived data and needs proof the R/D/I values are actual registration, rather than modeled affiliation. None of those uncertainties is cured by assuming a question or converting a censored cell to zero.

- RedBox publishes unweighted Ns of R 815, D 1,157, I 1,320. Party ID is unleaned and its independent group explicitly includes other-party and don't-know identifiers, including leaners. The first-choice vote includes those who already voted and omits ballot lean follow-up. The 3,292 observations combine statewide and district samples, with design effect 4.81.
- Change Research's spreadsheet gives *weighted* party bases R 363, D 358, pure I 103; the ballot explicitly includes candidate leans. Party groups use pure independents rather than all independent identifiers. The source Sheet was inspected in browser; xlsx export returned 401.
- Harper's Q26 asks respondents how they are registered and explicitly distinguishes that from self-identification. Its crosstabs give weighted rounded frequencies R 196, D 200, independent 171. Other registration (21) and unsure (12) are separate and are not included in the independent category. Individual cell weighted counts are fractional and the worksheet's total bases rounded. First-choice includes definitely/probably support, not the separate head-to-head ballot.
- ECU's original crosstab explicitly reports Democrat-to-Whatley as <1%, not zero. It provides no party subgroup N or party share. The censored value is null plus an explicit bound in JSON. Its September 30 field start does not disqualify the October 8 release.
- SurveyUSA's Q3 reports R/D/I support and composition of 36/38/25% among 643 likely or already-voted voters; subgroup Ns in JSON are derived from rounded composition, not reported counts. The source calls the group “Party Affiliation” but does not establish whether it is identity, registration, or something else, so it remains on hold.
- ActiVote publishes two-candidate R/D/I results and unweighted composition 16/41/42% of 400. Multiplying those rounded percentages by 400 produces estimates only. Its AAPOR disclosure says most respondents are linked to voter-file characteristics but does not establish that every party cell is actual registration rather than modeled affiliation. Kept for review, not accepted on inference alone.

Exclusions and access limits: YouGov's November 1 PDF, High Point's October 28 LV/RV memo, Elon's October 20 release/toplines/data charts, and Trafalgar's October 12 PDF did not provide a complete Senate vote by party table in the material inspected. Quantus' October 26 search-indexed article references a linked full report/crosstabs and mentions only two partisan cells; its live URL resolved to a generic homepage in browser and curl, and the report target could not be recovered. Quantus remains unresolved. ActiVote's in-window primary page was inspected and its crosstabs retained despite the basis issue. Candidate names are consistently Republican Michael Whatley and Democrat Roy Cooper; Elon 52/39 initial vs 54/43 forced-choice are alternative questions of one survey, not distinct waves.

The file `sources/harper_tables.txt` is a cell-addressed text extraction from the original workbook. `sources/surveyusa-main.html`, `sources/ecu.pdf`, `sources/redbox.pdf`, and `sources/harper.xlsx` preserve public originals. The record and inventory include source URLs and precise locations. All counts and party percentages remain source-specific and should not be equated to effective sample size.
