# Washington Post–Schar School follow-up

No additional in-window statewide Senate wave was found beyond the two already in the feed, so `polls.json` contains no new rows. The existing Maine poll was published October 9 and fielded September 30–October 5; the existing Kansas poll was published October 30 and fielded October 22–26. Their separate source URLs and the existing crosstab sheet links are in `inventory.json`. Kansas's alternative two-party question is the same survey and is not another independent wave.

The [Schar School index](https://schar.gmu.edu/washington-post-schar-school-poll), [Washington Post polling archive](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/polling/), targeted state and pollster searches, and the [99chambers pollster listing](https://99chambers.app/pollsters/george-mason-university-schar-school-of-policy-and-government-washington-post) were checked. The Schar index currently includes Maine but omits the later Kansas release; [the Post's own Kansas report](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/30/we-polled-kansas-democrats-wanted-talk-republicans-not-so-much/) independently confirms it. October Texas and Virginia Post–Schar surveys in the archive concern House districts, not statewide Senate samples. The Michigan Senate poll's [Schar page](https://schar.gmu.edu/news/2026-10/washington-post-schar-school-poll-finds-close-michigan-senate-race) carries `2026-10` in its URL but explicitly dates the release September 17, outside the publication window. This focused search does not establish that every available poll on the open internet has been found.
