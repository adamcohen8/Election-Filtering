NYTimes/Siena Poll of Likely Voters – Florida, Minnesota, and South Carolina

TOPICS:Alan WilsonAmy KlobucharAngie NixonAnnie AndrewsAshley MoodyByron DonaldsDarline GrahamDavid JollyFloridaGovernorJermaine JohnsonLisa DemuthMichele TafoyaMidterm ElectionsMinnesotaNew York TimesPeggy FlanaganSenateSiena PollSouth Carolina

Posted By: Siena Research Institute October 28, 2026

  • Florida:
    • U.S. Senate: Ashley Moody (R) 50% – Angie Nixon (D) 44%
    • Governor: Byron Donalds (R) 48% – David Jolly (D) 44%
  • Minnesota:
    • U.S. Senate: Peggy Flanagan (D) 54% – Michele Tafoya (R) 40%
    • Governor: Amy Klobuchar (D) 56% – Lisa Demuth (R) 38%
  • South Carolina:
    • U.S. Senate: Annie Andrews (D) 48% – Darline Graham (R) 48%
    • Governor: Alan Wilson (R) 51% – Jermaine Johnson (D) 44%
Florida Toplines Florida Crosstabs Minnesota Toplines Minnesota Crosstabs South Carolina Toplines South Carolina Crosstabs

“As the battles wage across the map for control of the U.S. Senate, South Carolina now jumps to centerstage. Republican Darline Graham, recently appointed to the senate, is locked in a toss-up race with Democrat Annie Andrews as Graham leads by less than one point,” said SRI’s Executive Director, Don Levy. “In Minnesota Democrat Peggy Flanagan enjoys a 14-point lead over Republican Michele Tafoya while in Florida, Republican Ashley Moody holds a 6-point advantage over Democrat Angie Nixon.”

Contact Information:

Siena Research Institute Director, Dr. Don Levy is available starting at 9:30 AM ET Wednesday, October 28th, to discuss the findings.

Levy can be reached at (518)-284-3551 or don.levy@reconmr.com to arrange for interviews in person or via phone.
