# Ohio Senate poll research, through November 1, 2026

Search covered five aggregators, original pollster reports and university repositories, dates by publication, and original public tables where accessible. It cannot prove all public polls were found. See inventory.json for 23 identified entries and source-specific exclusions. Wedgewood is excluded as a fabricated pollster.

Five survey waves have complete party crosstabs: RedBox (Oct 25 release), Verasight/VoteHub (Oct 22), Suffolk (Oct 28), Marist (Oct 20), and Fox (Oct 21); a fifth Suffolk wave was issued by the university October 2 but the Senate result appeared in Columbus Dispatch September 30, so first-publication eligibility is doubtful. The Suffolk release date should not be substituted uncritically for first release. Eight records include both Marist and Fox LV and RV alternatives, with matching sample IDs; do not count both within a wave.

RedBox reports 867 R, 1,166 D, 965 I as unweighted bases. Its independent category includes other party and don't know, explicitly; no lean recoding. Verasight's raw party n are 204 R, 219 D and 187 I, with effective n 182.0, 202.0, 147.3; 623 respondents are registered voters reweighted to likely turnout, rather than 531 actual respondents. Both tables are model-ready subject to model policy. Suffolk's tables show integer counts but do not establish weighting status; verify before treating as unweighted. Suffolk asks vote or lean toward. Marist likelihood is a weight, not a reported LV subset size: R/D/I visible values indicate at least 50 unweighted each under its suppression rule, with exact group Ns unknown. RV n are approximate floor of 1176 times rounded weighted composition, not actual group counts; RV alternative retains the same sample ID.

Both YouGov original PDFs show Senate results by age, sex, race, recalled 2024 vote and education but not party ID, so no calculated party split is asserted. BGSU repository PDF and CNN DocumentCloud returned 403 in both web and escalated curl. New York Times/Siena crosstabs could not be read via browser. Cook page returned 403. Fox publishes LV and RV party crosstabs but no subgroup counts or composition; an explicit >=100 reporting threshold is a lower bound, not an exact N. Its party definition needs further confirmation. Rasmussen and some other public poll releases lacked located complete party tables; these remain unresolved, not affirmatively proven unavailable. Marist's ballot calls the candidate Jon Husted, although a separate favorability question misspells him John; ballot name controls.

Original supporting PDFs saved under sources/. Ballot samples, source location, definitions, uncertainty, and weighted/unweighted issues are in polls.json. No central model files modified.
