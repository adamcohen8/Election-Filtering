## Working precision policy

Actual unweighted bases use the model's 1.5 design-effect default unless source information warrants more. Reported weighted or weighting-unspecified bases use 2.0, while rounded-composition estimates and published subgroup-suppression lower bounds use 3.0. Larger source-informed factors override these. Reported effective subgroup sizes are floored and use design effect 1 to avoid counting the same inflation twice. Fractional weighted bases are floored only for the model input; exact source numbers remain in provenance. These working factors are assumptions, not calibrated or guaranteed bounds on polling error. Published suppression thresholds supply conservative nominal information limits, never actual subgroup sizes. Unknown cells are not imputed.

The user-specified turnout uncertainty remains 1 percentage point per party share at one sigma (3 points at three sigma), with the sum-to-one covariance constraint. Polling precision choices above concern sampling/weighting, separately from turnout uncertainty. Persistent shared/pollster/survey errors remain in the existing methodology.
