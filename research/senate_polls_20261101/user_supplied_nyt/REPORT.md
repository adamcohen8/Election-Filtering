# User-supplied NYT/Siena tables

On November 1 the user supplied complete Alaska, Ohio, Texas, Kansas, Iowa, Florida and Minnesota candidate-by-party screenshots. Their rightmost PARTY ID columns were transcribed directly, selecting the combined Senate ballot including candidate leaners. Adjacent Senate-vote columns were not used. Individual `*-transcription.md` files record every cell and metadata limitations. Original screenshots remain in the chat; this folder contains manual text transcriptions, not downloaded primary originals.

Six surveys (Alaska, Ohio, Texas, Kansas, Florida and Minnesota) are accepted under the existing missing-count covariance policy: assumed 5pp SD per candidate/group cell and correlation -0.5 within each pair. Unknown subgroup counts remain null. This is a provisional precision assumption, not source-reported uncertainty. The full methodology can replace it if provided. Publication is October 3 for Ohio/Texas/Kansas/Iowa and conservatively bounded October 3–4 for Alaska. Field-end dates remain provisional from prior inventories; screenshots do not establish those dates. Unknown Ohio/Texas/Kansas/Iowa population, field starts and total samples are not invented. Alaska's LV designation remains labeled secondary-sourced and provisional. The ambient Alaska browser URL was not assigned to the other states.

Iowa uses fully visible reported subgroup counts R199/D163/I223 with weighting unspecified and working design effect 2. Kansas retains the third-party ballot: Graham has D2%, R<1%, I15%; major-party cells are not rescaled. Its sample-size row is clipped, so counts remain null.

Florida and Minnesota were published October 28. Florida has conflicting prior field-end dates October 25–26: chronology explicitly uses the later bound October 26 as a provisional assumption pending methodology, rather than claiming a verified date. Minnesota field end October 26 remains provisional from the prior inventory. Minnesota Tafoya among Democrats is censored <0.5%; the raw cell remains null with interval metadata, and the model uses a labeled 0.25% midpoint plus additional variance 0.0025 squared.

This adds seven waves to 89, bringing the accepted total to 96: Alaska three, Ohio ten, Texas twenty, Kansas seven, Iowa seven, Florida nine and Minnesota six. All seven requested NYT/Siena tables are now included, with the metadata and precision limitations stated above. Earlier access-block reports are historical; this package and central review decisions supersede their unresolved status for these seven supplied tables. No direct NYT browser request or image generation was performed.
