# New Hampshire Senate polling research, October 1–November 1, 2026

Six distinct eligible or potentially eligible waves have complete party-group support values and are preserved as candidates, not approved for automatic ingestion: one needs subgroup denominators and five need review. UMass Lowell/YouGov has self-reported-style **leaned Party ID** labeled as such, but no subgroup counts or composition found. NPA supplies registration figures with estimates based on weighted rounded shares, not measured subgroup counts. Saint Anselm supplies registration with published weighted frequencies. Its separate party-identification table uses an unexplained “Swing” category, so it cannot be assumed to be independents; no second row for this wave was created. Confirm both registration tables reflect actual registration, not modeled voter-file classifications.

Rasmussen's two waves have complete table figures and published composition. Estimated group sizes are floor(N × rounded published share): October 2–4, R 303 / D 342 / Independent-Other 332; October 20–23, R 310 / D 329 / Independent-Other 329. These are not observed counts. The party basis and treatment of leaners remain unspecified; both remain needs_review. Rasmussen states that it weights by party, but its general methodology does not establish how party was classified. The early Rasmussen primary article shows no release date, but an October 5 aggregator changelog already included the Oct 2–4 wave; the Oct 26 date for the later wave is from a secondary citation. Resolve these definitions and dates before ingestion.

AARP/Anselm’s October 6 release for September 28–29 is eligible by publication date. The recovered original contains a complete party-identification table and weighted frequencies, but does not state how party identification was obtained or whether leaners are included; it is marked needs_review. Its separate registration table is recorded as an alternative in the same wave and is not counted twice. YouGov's October 7 article and 12-page September 28–October 5 survey toplines do not supply the needed party crosstabs. The UMass/YouGov and YouGov standalone samples are separate surveys. NPA's alternate head-to-head question is not separately counted.

The inventory covers Call the Map, 270toWin, On Point Politics, Wikipedia, Decision Desk HQ, RealClearPolling, direct polling-house queries, and AARP.
