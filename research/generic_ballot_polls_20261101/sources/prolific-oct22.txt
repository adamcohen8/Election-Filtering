 Prolific Poll (US) | October 2026
 Full questionnaire: 2026 midterm generic ballot and political trackers


 Fieldwork                  21–22 October 2026

 Mode                       Online, self-administered; survey scripted in Qualtrics. About 4 minutes.

 Sample                     US adults on Prolific's opt-in online participant panel, invited using propensity-score sampling: a
                            model of how the panel differs from the US adult population on age, sex, race, education, region
                            and 2024 and 2020 presidential vote defined 25 groups, each invited in proportion to its population
                            share. 1,518 US adult citizens analysed (non-citizens and one duplicate session excluded).

 Profile data               2020 presidential vote is taken from Prolific's participant-level profile data and is not asked in this
                            survey. 2024 presidential vote from the profile was used to invite participants; it is also asked in the
                            survey (Vote2024).

 Randomisation              (1) Order of the parties in the generic-ballot question: half of respondents saw the Democratic
                            candidate listed first, half the Republican candidate first (even presentation). (2) Topic-choice
                            experiment: a random half of respondents were first asked which topic they would like to give
                            opinions on; the other half went straight to the political questions. See Section 2.

 Question order             As printed below, following the fielded survey flow: Introduction and consent, Topic-choice
                            experiment (random half), Political questions, About you, Additional questions, End.

 Source                     Transcribed directly from the fielded Qualtrics survey file. Numeric answer codes are omitted for
                            readability; question wording, option order and display logic are reproduced as fielded. Every
                            question required an answer.
