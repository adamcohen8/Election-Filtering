 Weighting                                          The sample was weighted according to gender, age, race, education,
                                                 U.S.  region,   2024  presidential  vote,   2020  election  turnout  and

                                                 registration  status.   Demographic  weighting  targets  come  from  the

                                                 of  weighting  was  estimated  to  be  48%  Harris  and  50%  Trump.
                                                 Baseline party identification is the respondent’s most recent answer
                                                 given around November 8,  2024,  and is weighted to the estimated
                                                 distribution  at  that  time  (31%  Democratic,  33%  Republican).   The
                                                 weights range from 0.052 to 6.574, with a mean of one and a standard
                                                 deviation of 0.981.

 Number of respondents                   1,053

 Margin of error                       ± 4.2 points (adjusted for weighting)

 Likely Voter Screen                          "Strict"  indicates  a  respondent  who  is  currently  registered  to  vote
                                                 and  indicated  that  they  "Definitely  will  vote".    "Loose"  indicates  a
                                                 respondent who is either currently registered to vote or indicated that
                                                 they plan to register before the election, and also indicated that they
                                                 either "Definitely will vote" or "Probably will vote".

 Survey mode                                     Web-based interviews

                                                 deviation of 0.981.
