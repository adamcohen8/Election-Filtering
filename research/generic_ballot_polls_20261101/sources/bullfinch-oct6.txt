National Policy Survey — October 2026
Toplines — The Independent Center 2026 Nationwide Voter Survey
Online survey of U.S. adults, n = 1,200, of whom 1,000 are registered voters (RV). Results are unweighted (pre-field flex quotas
balanced to census and data universe targets on gender, age, race/ethnicity, partisanship, education level and geography). Margin of
error ±3.1 pts (RV) / ±2.8 pts (adults) at 95% confidence. Totals may not sum to 100 due to rounding; multi-select questions sum to
more than 100; "<1" is a non-zero value under 0.5%. Survey fielded 10/01-06/2026.
