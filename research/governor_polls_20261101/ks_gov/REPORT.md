# Kansas governor polling research

Six distinct qualifying waves are recorded: five October releases and one September backfill. The separately supplied NYT/Siena table is a seventh and is not duplicated. This falls three short of ten: no additional complete matching-candidate tables were located. Search cannot prove completeness, and the unlocated Global Strategy Group tables could add October or displace the September selection.

CNN's governor table was recovered in ordinary Safari from the original DocumentCloud report. Its party breakdown includes Independents and other identifiers, and its ballot incorporates candidate leaners. First publication is bounded October 28–29, rather than assumed from the October 29 news report. SurveyUSA is not used for this race.

Emerson and Washington Post use party registration, including independent/other or all other registrations. Emerson fractional subgroup counts are weighted, not interviews. The Post does not supply subgroup counts; its whole-sample and subgroup margins cannot establish exact counts. RedBox publishes unweighted party-ID counts and weighted shares, and flags an unresolved public concern about authenticity without proof. On Point's sheet says party ID but labels the third column unaffiliated/other, while its release discusses registration; the classification is therefore labeled unspecified. Its likely-voter percentages are weighted, and party interview Ns are null because its displayed unweighted row covers all registered respondents. Its release and workbook disagree on field start by one day.

The Kansas Speaks Q23 asked about these candidates but explicitly withheld results due to lack of a likely-voter screen; its appendix cannot supply these votes. YouGov's governor crosstabs break down demographics and prior vote rather than party. The co/efficient and Global Strategy Group releases found contain toplines or partial breakdowns. Wedgewood is excluded, and ideology is never substituted for party. NYT browser site-safety block was respected; user-supplied cells are handled separately.
