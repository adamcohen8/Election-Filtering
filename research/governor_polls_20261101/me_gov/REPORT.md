# Maine governor poll research — November 1, 2026

Ten distinct waves are recorded, eight published October 1–30 and two September 24 backfills. Nine provide six exact printed candidate percentages; the October 27 Marist likely-voter table reports Democratic support for Charles as <1%, stored as a censored interval rather than a fabricated exact value. It requires the central interval policy.

Candidate A is Bobby Charles and B is Hannah Pingree. The named ballot differs: CBS, CNN, and UMass name only the major-party candidates; other surveys also name Rick Bennett. Party groups are not interchangeable: UMass uses leaned party ID; WaPo, CNN and Data for Progress combine independent/other; Suffolk uses party registration with minor parties combined. Weighted sample Ns are labeled as such, and missing Ns remain null. Marist October 27 reports 1,449 registered respondents weighted to a likely-voter distribution, not a raw likely-voter sample.
