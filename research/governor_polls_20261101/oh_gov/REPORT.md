# Ohio governor poll research, through November 1, 2026

Five October waves have complete governor party tables: RedBox, Verasight, Suffolk, Marist, and Fox. The separate user-supplied NYT/Siena screenshot counts as a sixth for the backfill target and is deliberately absent from polls.json. Two September backfill waves (Suffolk and Marist) are included, yielding eight total when the separate screenshot is counted; the requested target of ten was not established in the sources searched. These are seven records, one per wave, not eight separate records in this folder.

All values are support for Ramaswamy and Acton conditional on party, not Senate results. RedBox and Verasight report unweighted subgroup counts. Suffolk reports integer party bases but does not establish their weighting status; both Suffolk rows need review. Fox and October Marist lack exact LV subgroup bases. September Marist counts are explicitly approximate floors of 1,298 multiplied by rounded weighted party composition, not reported unweighted counts. RedBox independently groups other party and don't know with independents; Verasight retains independent leaners. Fox asks a two-candidate question with other voluntary; ballot-stage differences are retained.

Suffolk September governor topline was first published September 29, although the university grouping was dated October 2. Its local PDF also contains an ad question resembling a later wave; that anomaly and provenance need review before use. October Marist likelihood is a weight, and 1,176 is registered sample size, not a reported LV base. NYT field end and publication date remain provisional and are controlled by the separate user-supplied evidence.

Coverage included 270toWin, DDHQ, RealClearPolling, original Marist, Fox, Verasight, RedBox, Suffolk, YouGov, co/efficient, Echelon, On Point, and ActiVote releases, and the pre-existing Senate source and browser access research. Neither YouGov crosstab provides conditional party support. Echelon and co/efficient provide toplines only; On Point provides an independent split but no complete party table; ActiVote reports no party breakdown. CNN DocumentCloud and BGSU repository sources returned 403 during previous normal browser and download attempts. Cook and AARP were likewise inaccessible via source fetch; no cells were guessed. BGSU September and Fox August were checked for backfill, without an accessible complete party table. Wedgewood is excluded. The search cannot establish that every public poll has been found.

Original accessible PDFs already retained at the source paths in polls.json under the Senate research folder. September Marist's full original is linked directly in the record; table transcriptions and all locations are included for verification. No central model files were changed.
