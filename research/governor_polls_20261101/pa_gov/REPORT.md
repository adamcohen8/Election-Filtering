# Pennsylvania governor polling, as of November 1, 2026

Five qualifying waves with all six candidate-by-party percentages were recovered: ActiVote October 21 and Quinnipiac October 7, July 15, February 25, and October 1, 2025. Two were published during October 2026. Older waves are backfill; the target of ten has not been reached, and the inventory records unresolved candidates. This is not a claim that all eligible surveys have been found.

Each JSON row presents Garrity first and Shapiro second. ActiVote reports registered party and a forced two-candidate ballot; its group sample sizes are not recoverable exactly from rounded percentages. Quinnipiac includes leaners; October also includes already-voted respondents and the Libertarian candidate. Its Rep/Dem/Ind basis is labeled unspecified because the ballot tables do not define it. All unreported subgroup Ns are null. Original Quinnipiac PDFs and the ActiVote webpage are saved in sources/, alongside a transcription and original URLs.

F&M October summary and topline PDFs were downloaded and inspected: their four selected crosstabs address other questions. Its August, June and March summaries likewise contain no party-by-governor-vote tables. Bravo's October dashboard was inspected in Safari: the Republican party filter yielded Garrity 56% and Shapiro 24%, but the other two party filters were not recovered, leaving that wave incomplete. Its September release was located but no complete party ballot table verified. Z to A/Rust Belt Rising and Susquehanna are listed in poll aggregators but complete party tables remain unverified. NPA's August memo is a topline with party composition, not a party-by-ballot table. Muhlenberg's located October poll covers PA-07 rather than the state. On Point's Pennsylvania forecast is not a poll, and its fieldwork release list does not list Pennsylvania. No forecast values were substituted.

The [September Times/Inquirer/Siena likely-voter cross-tab](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/09/24/polls/times-inquirer-siena-pa-poll-lv-crosstabs.html) is a potential backfill source, but NYT browser access was blocked; its table has not been read. A user-provided image of its governor-by-party table would permit verification. See inventory.json for source-specific leads and limitations.
