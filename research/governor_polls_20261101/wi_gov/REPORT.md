# Wisconsin governor polling research

Six distinct qualifying waves were recovered: Wick and Marquette in October; Marquette in September and August; and TIPP in August and March. Each records all six support percentages for Tom Tiffany and David Crowley among Republicans, Democrats and independents. There are fewer than the requested ten; the search did not establish that all otherwise eligible polls have public party crosstabs.

Wick's October 28 release provides party ID and weighted subgroup bases in its original Q9 table, not unweighted subgroup sizes. Marquette's tables provide initial ballot choice and straight party ID with leaners retained among independents; table frequencies are weighted. TIPP's original spreadsheets provide underlying vertical fractions and unweighted subgroup counts, but the party classification is not specified in the inspected banner. These differences matter when comparing polls. The Marquette and TIPP rows are marked for review in the data.

October City Forward Collective/YouGov's October 15 public release reports the statewide ballot but does not supply six party cells in its four-page release. Rust Belt Rising/Z-to-A's October 9 release was identified, but complete party crosstabs were not recovered. Neither is included. The July Institute for Reforming Government/RMG release and memo contain coalition descriptions but not the complete required six cells. These remain open gaps, not evidence that such tables do not exist. Polls with different candidate matchups or party-primary questions do not qualify.

Sources and exact table locations for included rows are in polls.json. Original documents, including Wick's PDF and TIPP workbooks, are in sources/.
