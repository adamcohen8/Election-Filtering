# Texas governor research

Thirteen October 1–November 1 publication-window waves with complete Abbott/Hinojosa candidate-by-party tables are in polls.json, including two explicitly modeled voter-file party tables (Quantus and Pollfinity). There is one record per wave, using the first-choice ballot when available; CBS publishes a leaner-inclusive ballot. No pre-October backfill is needed. This is a research inventory, not proof all releases were located; several party tables remain inaccessible or unverified. Field end determines chronology; publication establishes eligibility.

Party categories differ. Emerson, Data for Progress, and ReconMR pool independents with other or third-party categories. UT and UMass group party leaners. Quantus and Pollfinity use modeled voter-file party rather than self-identification; Pollfinity's Non-Partisan group is provisionally mapped to independent, n=17. Subgroup counts marked weighted are not unweighted interviews. Hobby and Texas Southern counts are estimates derived from rounded composition, not exact subgroup counts. Marist LV, UMass, UT and ReconMR have missing subgroup bases. Do not apply any whole-poll margin of error to subgroups. On Point late original Substack publication is October 30, despite the prior Senate inventory's October 29 date; its workbook title has conflicting field dates but release/methodology say October 27.
