# Iowa governor polling research

Nine independent governor waves are in polls.json: five published in the October 1–November 1 window and four older waves. A tenth wave is the separate user-supplied October NYT/Siena governor table; do not duplicate it. CNN/SSRS governor party cells were read in its original DocumentCloud browser viewer after the direct PDF download returned HTTP 403. Continued discovery is needed: the audit is not proof every open-internet release has been found. Four older waves were selected in descending recency among presently verified full tables; if additional October or newer pre-October waves qualify, reapply the ten-wave selection rule and remove the oldest backfill as appropriate.

The Fox, CNN/SSRS, October Marist, Emerson and On Point October records use the governor ballot, not the Senate ballot. On Point's workbook labels the columns party ID, while its release refers to no-party registration; the party question is unresolved. Its apparent unweighted LV subgroup bases repeat the entire RV sample, so they are left null. Marist October's LV subgroup bases and LV total are not established; 1,100 is the RV interview count. Fox displays minimum subgroup thresholds, not exact counts. Emerson's fractional Ns are weighted counts, and its Independent/other registration group is pooled. September Marist's party composition is rounded and no exact subgroup counts are supplied. Full-poll margin of error is not a subgroup margin.

Source leads include 270toWin, Call the Map, Iowa Ledger, RealClearPolling and original publisher sources recorded in inventory.json. GOTV suppresses its nonpartisan group; NPA's governor memo omits Democratic candidate-by-party cells. Suffolk documents inspected are marginals; Cygnal September reports toplines. No inference from ideology, other contests, wrong candidate matchups, or suppressed cells is used. NYT access is explicitly site-safety blocked, and the separately user-supplied table is the sole NYT source. Wedgewood is excluded.
