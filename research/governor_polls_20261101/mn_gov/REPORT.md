# Minnesota governor polling research

Nine distinct source-backed waves are in polls.json: three published during October and six earlier waves, selected newest first among the complete matching-candidate tables located. The separately supplied October 28 NYT/Siena table counts as the tenth wave and is deliberately not duplicated. The January and February questions were hypothetical matchups at the time. This inventory is not proof that every qualifying release or more recent backfill has been located.

Each record contains governor ballot cells for Lisa Demuth and Amy Klobuchar, release date, field end, original source, table location, and evidence path. Independent/other is pooled where the source does so. Missing subgroup sizes remain null. SurveyUSA October subgroup sizes are estimates derived from rounded weighted composition; TIPP percentages are weighted while the subgroup counts are unweighted; Emerson reports weighted fractional counts. Full-poll margins of error are not subgroup margins of error.

The October State Navigate release provides toplines but no governor candidate-by-party table. Victory gives party margins only, which do not determine six support percentages. The available Morning Consult graphic has no such party table. A September co/efficient topline release was found, but complete party tables were not located; they could change the older selection if recovered. These sources are inventoried, not counted.

NYT access was subject to a browser site-safety block; no alternative access was attempted. Its user-supplied table is maintained separately. No ideological breakdown is substituted for party, no separate first-choice/leaner versions of one wave are duplicated, and no unsupported Wedgewood data are included.
