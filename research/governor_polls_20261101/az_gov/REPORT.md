# Arizona governor polling: October 1–November 1, 2026

Ten confirmed distinct surveys provide all six requested Andy Biggs/Katie Hobbs by-party cells: seven have confirmed target-window release dates and three are older backfills (HighGround August 24, Quantus August 21, Noble August 20). ASU/BSP remains an eleventh raw record held for uncertain first-publication date. Its PDF says September and metadata shows September 9 creation, while October 6 is the first reporting identified; the record leaves publication date null and bounds it from September 4 to October 6. Its placement could alter the backfill order once its release is established.

The exact six ballot cells come from original question tables or the pollster's labeled chart, not from statewide toplines or ideology. RedBox and Wick explicitly provide party identification. RedBox's unweighted respondent bases apply to the broad party-ID independents; its results exclude ballot leaners, and the overall design effect 3.77 and subgroup-specific MOEs should not be confused. Wick labels its weighted subgroup bases; they are not unweighted interview counts. HighGround identifies GOP, Democrat, PND and Other separately in its voter-file sample; PND is used as the no-party registration proxy and Other is not silently combined. Its three subgroup bases are unavailable. GrayHouse explicitly calls REP, DEM and UNF registration and defines UNF as unaffiliated and other parties; subgroup bases are unavailable in its table.

Noble labels party without documenting whether it is identification, registration or modeled; its reported group bases are weighted. KStrat's label is similarly unresolved and group Ns are null. American Encore explicitly identifies an unresolved inconsistency: its governor party columns combined at the claimed 39/32/29 turnout composition give different totals than its reported topline; a different 33.5/35.7/30.8 mix reproduces its rows. Retain the published subgroup figures with review status and do not treat either mix as observed respondent counts. ASU combines independent and other in a party column, and exact party measure and ballot subgroup Ns remain unspecified.

Quantus labels its ballot table registered party while also saying party ID is modeled from the voter file; its subgroup bases are weighted. Its unaffiliated/DTS column is retained as a narrow proxy; a separate independent/other column exists and is not silently pooled. The evidence is in `sources/`, including the separately hosted original Quantus PDF, original HighGround graphics, GrayHouse PDF and Noble workbook.

Search covered primary Arizona pollster and sponsor releases, statewide polling indexes, and several older waves. Unavailable or partial October releases, including GrayHouse October 28–29 and October 6–8, GQR, Equis, and other studies, are listed in `inventory.json`; these are source gaps rather than proof that no party table exists. In particular, an earlier ASU release or a more recent eligible crosstab would change the backfill order. All dates and field periods are distinct; no Wedgewood, wrong matchup, ideology proxy, topline-only result or duplicated survey variant is included.
