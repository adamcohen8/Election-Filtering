Hobbs–Biggs ballot
01 Hobbs leads, narrowly
49.1 to 43.2 on the ballot, 53.2 to 46.8 on the two-way. The lead 
is real but sits close to the survey’s margin of error.
02 The electorate is already decided
Only 6.0 percent are undecided and 2.4 percent are unsure on 
re-election. Both candidates are net-defined, with Hobbs at 
+1.5 favorability and Biggs at −1.4.
03 Re-elect tracks the ballot
50.0 percent say Hobbs deserves re-election against 47.6 who 
say it is time for a new person, a net of +2.4.
04 Age is the sharpest divide
Hobbs takes 80.6 percent of the two-way among voters 18 to 
29 and 44.2 among those 65 and over.
05 Geography sets the map
Urban voters back Hobbs 56.0 to 40.4; rural and town voters 
back Biggs 47.6 to 32.8.
06 The environment is a headwind
Presidential approval stands at 43.4 percent against 55.4 
disapproving, a net of −12.0 and considered the backdrop for 
every reading in this survey.

M E T H O D O L O G Y
How this survey was conducted
P OP U L A T I O N
Likely voters in the 2026 Arizona general election. Likely-voter status was set 
by the Quantus Insights turnout model, which estimates each voter’s 
probability of participating from vote history, demographics, partisan-lean 
indicators and voter-file geography. Turnout scores define and weight the 
population. A screening question was administered for the online sample 
only.
U NI V E R S E
Active registered voter-file records with two or more general elections voted.
F I E L D  P ER I O D
August 14 to August 17, 2026.
M O D E
Online panel and SMS-to-mobile cell interviews.
S A M PL I NG
Stratified random sampling from the eligible universe, matched to 
available contact records.
W E I G H T I N G
Iterative proportional fitting, or raking, to benchmarks for the Arizona 
likely general-election electorate on seven dimensions: sex, age, race, 
education, party, metro type and media market. Race comes from the 
voter-file field. Weights were trimmed to limit the influence of individual 
respondents and renormalised each iteration.
M A R G I N  O F ER R O R
±4.1 percentage points at the 95 percent confidence level, design-
adjusted, on the full sample of 780. Subgroup margins are larger.
R E PO R T I N G
Crosstab bases are weighted to the electorate profile.. Town is folded 
into rural and the balance of state into Tucson; Black, Asian and Other 
are reported as one group.
R O U ND I N G
Percentages may not total 100 due to rounding. Full precision is retained 
in the underlying results.
For questions or inquiries email contact@quantusinsights.org or visit 
quantusinsights.org.
