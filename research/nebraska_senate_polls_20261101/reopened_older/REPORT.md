# Nebraska Senate: older-source re-audit

One additional qualifying poll was located: Lake Research Partners, December 11–17, 2025, publicly attributed to January 22, 2026. It has a two-candidate Ricketts–Osborn ballot by registration, not party ID. The source file is saved with its SHA-256 in inventory.json.

First-choice candidate shares, before leaners, are obtained by adding the displayed rounded strong and not-so-strong cells. Among registered Republicans / Democrats / unaffiliated and other, respectively, Ricketts receives 73 / 6 / 24%; Osborn receives 18 / 88 / 63%. Rounding can yield a one-point discrepancy against an unrounded aggregate. The table's lean-inclusive aggregates are Ricketts 75 / 7 / 27 and Osborn 19 / 89 / 66. The registration group merges unaffiliated and third-party registrants, so it is a proxy for party ID. The group counts 483 / 234 / 183 are printed but their weighting status is not specified; they are retained in the evidence excerpt and subgroup n fields are null.

The February Impact memo and September GBAO memo provide partial party splits and cannot satisfy six cells; they are archived here with hashes. The September SurveyUSA and May Tavern matches were included previously and are not duplicates here. A SurveyUSA 2024 document has separate Fischer–Osborn and Ricketts–Love races and is excluded.
