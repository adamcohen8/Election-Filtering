# Nebraska Senate polling research — November 1, 2026

Three verified polls contain all six conditional candidate support cells: one published October 1–November 1 and two older backfills. Fewer than ten qualifying polls were located; the inventory records the material public leads screened. Pete Ricketts is Republican and Dan Osborn is Independent. The six cells use each party group as denominator and are not two-candidate-normalized.

| Poll / publication | Basis | R: Ricketts / Osborn | D: Ricketts / Osborn | I or equivalent: Ricketts / Osborn |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| On Point, Oct 30 | Registration; modeled undecided allocation | 77.43 / 19.08 | 5.97 / 88.34 | 22.98 / 73.13 |
| SurveyUSA, Sep 14 | Party affiliation | 78 / 14 | 2 / 87 | 17 / 68 |
| Tavern, May 11 | Self-identified party | 76 / 14 | 2 / 92 | 20 / 62 |

On Point's nonpartisan/other category includes third-party registrations, so it is a registration proxy, not interchangeable with party identity. Its ballot assigns undecided voters using modeled responses; it is not a straightforward reported-intention crosstab. Its published unweighted ballot bases are 546/630/338. SurveyUSA reports rounded composition rather than subgroup n; subgroup n is null. Tavern's reported group n does not state whether weighted. The chosen central missing-n policy is the parent analysis's five-percentage-point covariance treatment, not an invented count. Samples, ballot options, methods, and dates differ and these three polls do not establish an exhaustive census of all polls.

NYT/Siena's October 31 crosstabs were not accessed because the parent reported a site policy denial. Do not infer subgroup cells from toplines. YouGov's two linked releases, the NPA memos, co/efficient, and Concord's publicly viewed memo do not provide all six cells. GBAO's DocSend source returned 403 and was not bypassed. Tavern is a two-candidate hypothetical before the final ballot. Source documents and SHA-256 hashes are in inventory.json.
